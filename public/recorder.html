Conversion Summary

Supported Recorder steps and important limitations

The converter handles common Recorder steps including viewport changes, navigation, click/double-click, hover, form value changes, key down/up, scrolling, wait-for-element, wait-for-expression, and close.

Network emulation and custom Recorder steps are emitted as TODO comments. Nested frame/target recordings are flagged for review. Recorder selectors are converted to Playwright locators using a practical preferred selector from the alternatives in the JSON.

Always review recorded form values before saving. Password-like fields are parameterized when detection is enabled, but other sensitive values may still exist in the recording.
